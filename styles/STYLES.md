# MotionKit · Style library (12 styles)

Each card is a complete recipe: pick one style per film (or one primary + one accent). Tokens are starting values — swap the brand's colours/fonts into the same roles. Fonts listed are HyperFrames-bundled unless marked.

Card fields: **Essence · Refs · Ground · Palette · Type · Protagonist · Signature moves · Carriers (from grammar/01) · Camera · Pacing · Music · Avoid**.

Every style obeys `grammar/08-camera.md` (camera moving ≥ 65 % of frames) and `grammar/09-polish-bar.md` (tight glows, round corners at every frame, no UI pops).

## S1 · Ember Flow  🔥  *(sample A)*
- **Essence:** one warm glowing element morphs through every idea on black; two-word poetic lines with an italic serif keyword. Calm, intimate, premium.
- **Refs:** v52 ⭐⭐⭐, v39, v61, v25, v04, v47.
- **Ground:** `#050403` with a faint warm radial pool (`#1A0D06` 30%) and 4% grain; optional cream light act `#F6EFE6`.
- **Palette:** ember `#FFB547`, orange `#FF7A1A`, coral `#FF4F6D`, cream `#F6EFE6`, ink-on-cream `#1C1410`.
- **Type:** Inter 400 (lead words, 3.2% height) + **EB Garamond Italic** for the keyword; persistent tiny caption at top.
- **Protagonist:** an ember dot with bloom (core white-yellow, halo orange).
- **Signature moves:** splash + ripple rings · ember → sun with lens flare · ring of beads wobbling into a flower blob (SVG path morph) · ring → line → gradient pill → card with reflection · beads climbing concentric arcs · white iris to cream act · hanging-string pills (bezier) that heat up · ∞ comet trail · metaballs merging · logo forms from the blobs.
- **Carriers:** guide object, shape morph, iris (dark↔light), persistent title.
- **Camera:** follow the ember as it falls (tilt down), slow push on the sun, orbit the scattered sparks (they sit at depths −380…+260), push in on the blob, orbit the card 17° → −9°, truck down the hanging string. (Lumen v2)
- **Pacing:** ~3–4 s per state, 0 cuts, median motion very low. **Music:** `v52-ember-cooking` (ambient), cut on sections 11.5 / 24.5.
- **Avoid:** more than one hue family; hard edges; fast cuts; sans-only type.

## S2 · Morph Continuum  🫧  *(sample B)*
- **Essence:** a single centred flat icon continuously morphs into the next object for the whole film — the story is told by metamorphosis. Pure Bentomotion.
- **Refs:** v06 ⭐⭐⭐, v37 ⭐⭐⭐ (illustrated variant), v46 shape strip, v34 globe→pill.
- **Ground:** constant flat canvas — lavender `#E8E3EF` (or brand pastel). Never changes.
- **Palette:** greys `#C9C6D0 / #6E6E73 / #1D1D1F`, white `#FFFFFF`, plus 3–5 saturated "product colours" (e.g. `#FF6B57 #4C7DFF #2BC48A #FFC53D #9B5CFF`).
- **Type:** almost none. One small Inter 600 label per chapter max; end card.
- **Protagonist:** the object itself, centred, 25–40% frame height.
- **Signature moves:** silhouette bridge (circle family), satellite bubbles merging into rings, desaturate→reshape→recolour, continuous orbit carrier (group keeps rotating while members change), stroke draw-on details, multiplication (one → grid/fan of variants), scale-through (object grows past camera, its interior is the next canvas).
- **Carriers:** shape morph, match by rhyme, scale-through.
- **Camera:** gentle roll ±2–5°, push-ins on each state, a kick on the key onset, a dive into the phone (z +420) and pull-back to the hub, stars on a deep layer for parallax. Objects stay flat-facing (no big yaw on flat icons). (Nook v2)
- **Pacing:** a new state every 1.5–3 s; never a hold > 1 s; ease-in-out everywhere; overshoot only on "pops". **Music:** `v06-bentomotion-ambient`.
- **Avoid:** text-heavy beats, background changes, photos.

## S3 · Neon Rim Glow  🌈  *(sample C)*
- **Essence:** black void; rounded UI shapes outlined by moving spectral rim-light (Apple-Intelligence edge glow); everything bleeds light.
- **Refs:** v43 ⭐⭐⭐, v11 ⭐⭐, v35, v25, v55.
- **Ground:** `#000000` + a coloured haze band at 15–25% opacity near the active element.
- **Palette:** `#FF8A2A → #FF2BD6 → #7B3CFF → #2E6BFF → #29E3FF` (conic), white text `#F5F5F7`, dim `#6B6B72`.
- **Type:** Inter 500 small (2.6–3.4% height) inside pills; one bigger word per beat; triangle/outline cursor.
- **Protagonist:** a pill/card with travelling rim-light; or a white guide dot.
- **Signature moves:** rim-light conic rotating around a pill · card grows from pill, light runs along the border · macro into the glowing corner · glass orb with "…" · ring of outline circles with one glowing · neon eye (two arcs) with pupil = pill · Siri multi-sine waveform · light-trail cursor · frame → line → wave chain · light tubes tracing an L corner meeting in a 4-point star → logo.
- **Carriers:** shape morph (frame→line→wave), guide object, element-becomes-world (card corner).
- **Camera:** high-angle push onto the first pill, orbit the scattered chips (depth −260…+230, DOF), tilt 26° to see the ring of hours as a dial, slow push into the eye, swing 17° round the card, dive through the star. (Aura v2)
- **Pacing:** 2–3 s per idea; vertical-first. **Music:** `v43-youmotion-neon` or `v11-youmotion-thought`.
- **Avoid:** flat fills, light backgrounds for long, more than 2 elements on screen.

## S4 · Breathing-Gradient SaaS  💠  *(sample D)*
- **Essence:** white canvas with soft blue/brand gradients pooling and breathing; kinetic sentences with one bold/coloured payoff word; floating glass UI; toggle before/after; CTA.
- **Refs:** v27 ⭐⭐, v17 ⭐, v41, v58, v31 (silk variant), v29.
- **Ground:** `#F7F7FC` + 3 breathing blobs (`#7D86F0` 35%, `#2639C9` 25%, `#C9CCF8` 50%), blur 120 px. Dark acts: `#05061A` with blue rising glow.
- **Palette:** ink `#0E1030`, royal `#2639C9`, periwinkle `#7D86F0`, mist `#C9CCF8`, success `#2BC48A`.
- **Type:** Outfit / Poppins 500 for sentences, 700 for key noun; **script/italic accent** for brand words (EB Garamond Italic or a script woff2).
- **Protagonist:** the sentence + a small guide dot / 3D cursor.
- **Signature moves:** tracking-camera sentence · highlight-pill keyword · level glass cards at depth the camera flies through (no tilted stickers, no pops) · pills marquee rows (opposite directions) with centre pill selected · fan-out lines from a node · toggle OFF→ON resolves the problem cards · odometer stat · phone mockup in 3D tilt with cursor · slot word for benefits · logo + CTA pill.
- **Carriers:** element-becomes-world, colour flood (light↔dark), guide object, carry-across.
- **Camera:** one world — fly through a field of level glass cards (z −1100 → +800), arrive at the product plane, pull back through the same cards (now resolved), push in as the knob becomes the dashboard, orbit it, truck right to the claim, tilt to the network, track down the toasts, rack focus for the closing lines. (Tally v2)
- **Pacing:** 2–3 s beats, cut layout on the bar. **Music:** `v00-sample-this-100bpm` (100 BPM: beat 0.6 s, bar 2.4 s).
- **Avoid:** stock dashboards with lorem ipsum; same entrance twice in a row.

## S8 · Single-Line Story
- **Essence:** one continuous coloured line (+ dot) draws the entire film: underlines words, traces silhouettes, threads icons, writes the logo; finale pulls back to reveal the whole drawing.
- **Refs:** v57 ⭐⭐⭐, v11, v36. **Palette:** cream `#F8F3EC` / black `#0B0B0B` alternating, line `#F26A2E`. **Type:** Inter 400 tiny + script logo.
- **Moves:** read-along underline, silhouette trace, pixel icons on the curve, wave with letters on crests, zoom-out journey map.
