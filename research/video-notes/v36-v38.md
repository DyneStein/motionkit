- 3–5.5s (dense strip): daisy floats on blue **grid paper** → a **soap bubble forms around it** with a starburst pop flash → bubble wobbles (jelly squash/stretch) with the daisy inside on a string → camera drops: bubble shrinks to a small ball sitting between **stacked yellow discs** (like stacked plates/lens slices) that slide apart vertically = *parallax disc stack descent* → whole thing turns into a **giant textured sun** with the blue ball rolling under its rays.

- 15.5–20s: cup on table, coffee **drains** (liquid level ring shrinks), camera slowly pushes; napkin "thanks for watching" handwritten.
- **Morph techniques:** bubble encapsulation & pop; **squash-and-stretch on every contact**; shape-as-portal (globe whirlpool); colour-infection merge; object rhymes (circle motif carried through daisy centre → bubble → ball → sun → globe → ball → clock → target → cup) ⭐ *shape rhyme = invisible transitions*; textured grain on everything.
- **Lessons:** for liquid feel, every transition reuses a **circle** and every impact has squash/stretch and a soft wobble.
